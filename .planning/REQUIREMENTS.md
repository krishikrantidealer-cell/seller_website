# EBS BioCrop Science - Requirements Specification

Derived from the Agricultural E-Commerce Research Paper Blueprint & Benchmark:

## 1. Brand & Visual Requirements
- **FR-BR-01:** Strictly use EBS BioCrop Science branding; remove all third-party benchmark brand names.
- **FR-BR-02:** Use the EBS color palette (Leaf/Primary Green `#1B5E20`, Dark Forest `#0F3E16`, Harvest Amber `#E65100`, Brand Red `#B71C1C`).
- **FR-BR-03:** Provide clear Hindi subtitles and iconography for key farmer-facing actions (`फसल डॉक्टर`, `दैनिक मंडी भाव`, `टोल फ्री आर्डर`, `कैश ऑन डिलीवरी`).

## 2. Technical Search & Catalog
- **FR-CT-01:** Search by active chemical technical ingredient (e.g. *Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC*, *Emamectin Benzoate*, *Bio-NPK*, *Trichoderma Viride*).
- **FR-CT-02:** Quick Technical filter tags beneath the search bar for fast 1-click mobile search.
- **FR-CT-03:** Multi-category taxonomy (Crop Protection, Fertilizers & Bio, Seeds, Spray Equipment, Organic/IPM, Pest Traps).

## 3. Product Display & Agronomic Intelligence
- **FR-PD-01:** Pack-size selector pills (e.g. 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1L) with real-time recalculation of MRP, selling price, savings amount, and per-unit cost.
- **FR-PD-02:** CIBRC statutory toxicity triangle indicator (Green = Safe/Bio, Blue = Moderate, Yellow = High).
- **FR-PD-03:** Dosage guidance per 15L spray pump & per acre.
- **FR-PD-04:** Resilient 404 image placeholder fallback for every image visual.

## 4. Farmer Diagnostic & Advisory Tools
- **FR-AG-01:** **Crop Doctor ("फसल डॉक्टर")**: Interactive symptom and disease selector by crop (Cotton, Chilli, Tomato, Wheat, Soybean) showing instant diagnosis, recommended active chemicals, and 1-click add to cart.
- **FR-AG-02:** **Acreage & Dosage Calculator**: Input field size (acres) and spray equipment to compute required bottles/kilograms automatically.
- **FR-AG-03:** **APMC Mandi Bhav Ticker**: Real-time modal and banner showing commodity market prices (Cotton, Soybean, Mustard, Wheat, Chana).

## 5. Assisted Ordering & Checkout
- **FR-CO-01:** Floating / sticky "Call Toll-Free 1800-203-1425" & "WhatsApp Order" quick action bar.
- **FR-CO-02:** Cash on Delivery (COD) and 100% Genuine GST Bill guarantee.
- **FR-CO-03:** Slide-over cart drawer with free delivery progress indicator and item quantities.
